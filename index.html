About

Physics is a lot more fun once it stops being homework.

We're a group of students who meet weekly to work through mechanics problems you won't find on a normal problem set — the kind that show up on the F=ma exam, and the kind that just make you go "wait, why does that work?" No prior competition experience required, just curiosity and a willingness to get a problem wrong a few times before you get it right.

In keeping with the school's mission of forming students for others, we also run an outreach event each year — visiting a local elementary or middle school to show younger students cool physics concepts firsthand.
