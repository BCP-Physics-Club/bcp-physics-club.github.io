About

Physics is a lot more fun once it stops being homework.

We're a group of students who meet weekly to work through problems you won't find on a normal problem set — the kind that show up on the F=ma exam, USAPhO, and IPhO, and the kind that just make you go "wait, why does that work?" No prior competition experience required, just curiosity and a willingness to get a problem wrong a few times before you get it right.

Join

No prerequisites. Just show up.
